## Main Purpose

After knowing how good Cambly can be and be part of its students team, it's time to take another step at my English learning journey! The second week is prepared to be a continuation of the exploration started previous week by knowing more people, talking to many other teachers and writing about those experiences.

___

## Classes

### Cambly Class 00 - 10m

My English class journey at Cambly started on a promotional situation involving a link/voucher sent by a friend (who actualy is a Cambly student) allowing me to do a free 10 minutes conversation with any teacher. So, I just explored everything at Cambly's website and did my first free English conversation. The details of this experience can be found below:

| **Platform** | **Teacher** | **Teacher's nationality** | **Time** | **Date** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Cambly | Stuart Casey | Ireland | 10m | 03/10/2020 (Tuesday) |

It was a very good conversation with teacher Stuart Casey and it was a very important start that made me see how is my English level. I started a little shy and I think it was pretty normal given the first class scenario, but teacher Stuart handled the situation very well and soon enough I found myself trying my best to communicate with him. We talked about technology and Stuart mentioned [Boston Dynamics](https://www.bostondynamics.com/) as a really impressive project that creates robots in a way we are not used to see. At the beginning, I didn't remember Boston Dynamics but as a long as a did a google search, I remembered seeing this at television and other social media content.

At the end, I think it was a really good start and I also pretend to do more classes with teacher Stuart.

### Cambly Class 01 - 30m

So, after taking the 10 minutes free class at Cambly, I registered for a 3-days/week plan to keep my goals at improving my English. After the registration, I started looking for a new teacher. My plan at this moment is to take conversations with a lot of teachers for knowing more people and retain new characteristics that can help me at my journey. In this search I found teacher Ev and the details of this conversation will be explained just below:

| **Platform** | **Teacher** | **Teacher's nationality** | **Time** | **Date** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Cambly | Ev | Canada | 10m | 06/10/2020 (Thusrday) |

Tutor Ev was very kind and his approach was really impressive. We started talking about a Netflix series called Sons of Anarchy because of a t-shirt I was wearing and suddenly we were speaking about many other subjects, like food and tips for learning English. Tutor Ev worked many years as an Engineering and he traveled a lot for many other countries, including Brazil.

One thing that Tutor Ev said that it was very useful is a recommendation for reading, practicing speaking and text interpretation: the website [Engoo daily news](https://engoo.com/app/daily-news). This website is made by a lot of articles from many topics and areas that can be used by students for practicing English. Tutor Env recommended reading out loud some of the articles for practicing the speaking and the right pronunciation of words. The articles at the Engoo platform were divided by level, from 1 to 8, and can be chosen by area of interest.
